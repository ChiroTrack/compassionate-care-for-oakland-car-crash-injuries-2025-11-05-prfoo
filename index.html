About Pre Chiropractic

Pre Chiropractic was founded with a simple but powerful mission: to provide Oakland residents with compassionate, expert chiropractic care following car accidents and injuries. Our clinic was born from the recognition that accident victims need more than just clinical treatment—they need understanding, support, and genuine care throughout their healing journey. Over the years, we've built a reputation as Oakland's trusted partner in recovery, combining evidence-based techniques with the human touch that makes all the difference in patient outcomes.

Our core values center on compassion, integrity, and excellence. We believe that every patient deserves to be heard, respected, and treated as an individual with unique needs. We're committed to staying at the forefront of chiropractic innovation while maintaining the personal touch that our patients value. Our bilingual team reflects the diversity of Oakland and ensures that cultural and language differences never stand in the way of exceptional care. When you choose Pre Chiropractic, you're choosing a partner who genuinely cares about your recovery and your return to a healthy, active life.

First, ensure everyone's safety and call emergency services if needed. Document the accident scene with photos if possible. Seek medical attention promptly, even if you feel fine—some injuries appear days later. Contact your insurance company and then reach out to Pre Chiropractic to schedule an evaluation. Early intervention can prevent long-term complications and help you recover faster. We recommend scheduling a consultation within 48-72 hours of the accident for optimal results.

Yes, we work with most insurance providers, including auto insurance, health insurance, and workers' compensation. We handle the billing process to make it as convenient as possible for you. Our staff can verify your coverage and explain your benefits before treatment begins. If you have questions about your specific insurance plan, please contact us at prechiropractic@gmail.com or call our office. We're committed to making quality care affordable and accessible.

Recovery time varies significantly depending on the severity of your injuries, your overall health, and how well you follow our treatment recommendations. Minor injuries might resolve in 2-4 weeks, while more serious conditions could take 8-12 weeks or longer. During your initial consultation, we'll provide a personalized timeline based on your specific situation. Most patients see noticeable improvement within the first 2-3 weeks of treatment. We'll monitor your progress regularly and adjust your plan as needed.
